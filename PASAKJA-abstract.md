# ABSTRACT

**PASAKJA: A WEB-BASED COMMUNITY TRANSPORTATION BOOKING & DISPATCHING SYSTEM**

JUDY-ANN P. PUNO, DAPHNIE DANE V. SUPERO, LEEROSE T. GUMA  
Bucas Grande Foundation College, Socorro, Surigao del Norte  
Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, 2025

---

Community transportation in Socorro, Surigao del Norte has long relied on manual and informal booking practices, resulting in inefficient dispatching, unclear fare structures, long passenger waiting times, and a lack of accountability between drivers and commuters. This study presents Pasakja: A Web-Based Community Transportation Booking and Dispatching System, developed to address these challenges through a centralized, role-based digital platform.

The system was built using Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, NextAuth, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui, with Cloudinary for profile image management and PayMongo for GCash-based online payment integration. It serves three user roles — Passenger, Driver, and Administrator — each with a dedicated, access-controlled dashboard. Key features include GPS-assisted ride booking, automated fare computation by service zone, real-time booking status tracking, driver verification workflows, earnings monitoring, an emergency SOS alert system, and administrative reports and analytics.

The system was evaluated using the ISO/IEC 9126 software quality model across six quality criteria: functionality, efficiency, usability, reliability, maintainability, and portability. Results confirmed that the system successfully digitizes and organizes community transportation operations, improving service accessibility, operational transparency, and passenger safety. Pasakja demonstrates how modern web technologies can be applied to solve practical, community-level transportation challenges in a local Philippine context.

**Keywords:** *web-based system, ride booking, transportation dispatching, GPS tracking, fare computation, community transportation, Next.js, Philippines*
